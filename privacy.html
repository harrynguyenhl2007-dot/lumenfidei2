Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

LumenFidei Tools ("the app") is an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel LumenFidei Explain. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app uses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app uses YouTube API Services to access the owner's own channel: channel and video statistics (views, subscribers, watch time) and video metadata, and to upload videos as private.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to report on and manage the owner's own channel.
  • OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's private computer.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting data

Revoking access stops all data collection. Stored data is deleted on request or within 30 days after access is revoked.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel owner via the YouTube channel above.
